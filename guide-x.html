How to trend Heartbound on X.

Effective Trending Guide on X:

1. The “15/15” Posting rule:

  • In the first 15 minutes, post text-only tweets (at least 15 characters).
  • DO NOT use emojis, images, videos, or special characters.
  • After the first 15 minutes, you can post tweets with attached images or videos.

2. Interaction Strategy:

  • Regularly interact with large accounts (OFFICIAL PAGES, FANBASES, TREND) and Top Tweets.
  • Preferably use one language when participating in a trend.
  • Interact and reply back and forth in comments.

3. Account Requirements:

The account needs a minimum number of followers (100–500) and regular activity.

Note: Posted content must be at least 15 characters long, including the official hashtags and key-words, and MUST NOT be left blank to avoid being flagged as spam.
